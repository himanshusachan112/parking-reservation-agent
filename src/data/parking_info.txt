## General Information

ParkSmart is a modern automated parking facility located in the heart of downtown. Our parking complex offers 500 parking spaces spread across 5 floors, including dedicated spots for electric vehicles, disabled persons, and VIP members.

Our facility is equipped with state-of-the-art security systems including 24/7 CCTV surveillance, automated license plate recognition, and on-site security personnel. We provide both short-term and long-term parking solutions for cars, motorcycles, and small vans.

## Parking Space Types

1. Standard Parking Spaces (350 slots)
   - Floors 1-3
   - Suitable for sedans, hatchbacks, and small SUVs
   - Maximum vehicle dimensions: 5.0m x 2.0m x 1.8m (L x W x H)

2. Large Vehicle Spaces (80 slots)
   - Floor 4
   - Suitable for large SUVs, pickup trucks, and small vans
   - Maximum vehicle dimensions: 6.0m x 2.5m x 2.2m (L x W x H)

3. Electric Vehicle Spaces (40 slots)
   - Floor 2 (Section EV)
   - Equipped with Level 2 charging stations
   - Tesla Supercharger compatible
   - Charging is included in the parking fee

4. Disabled Parking Spaces (20 slots)
   - Floor 1 (closest to elevators)
   - Extra-wide spaces for wheelchair accessibility
   - Requires valid disability permit

5. VIP/Reserved Spaces (10 slots)
   - Floor 1 (Premium Section)
   - Covered, extra-wide, near the main exit
   - Available only with monthly subscription

## Booking Process

To reserve a parking space at ParkSmart, follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name and last name
2. Provide your vehicle registration number (license plate)
3. Specify the reservation period (start date & time, end date & time)
4. Our system will check availability for your requested period
5. An administrator will review and confirm your reservation
6. You will receive a confirmation with your spot number

Important Booking Rules:
- Reservations must be made at least 1 hour in advance
- Maximum reservation period: 30 days
- Cancellations are free up to 2 hours before the start time
- Late cancellations incur a 50% fee
- No-shows are charged the full amount
