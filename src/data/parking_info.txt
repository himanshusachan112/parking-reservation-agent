## Parking Categories (Descriptions)

### Standard Parking
Covered parking bays suitable for hatchbacks, sedans, and compact SUVs.
Located on floors P1, P2, and P3. Features CCTV monitoring, elevator access,
and smart slot-guidance indicators. Maximum vehicle size: 5.0 m × 2.0 m × 1.8 m.

### Large Vehicle Parking
Extra-wide lanes and high roof clearance for large SUVs, MUVs, pickup trucks,
corporate vans, and tempo travelers. Dedicated floor P4 with easy maneuvering area.
Maximum vehicle size: 6.5 m × 2.5 m × 2.5 m.

### EV Charging Parking
Dedicated zone for electric vehicles on floor P2. Equipped with Level 2 AC chargers
and DC fast-charge ports. Charging cost is included in the parking fee. Supported
vehicles include Tata EV, MG ZS EV, Tesla, BYD, Ather, and Ola Electric.
Charging sessions monitored through the smart EV management system.

### VIP Premium Parking
Exclusive covered spots on the ground floor closest to the main entrance.
Reserved for corporate executives, premium members, and invited guests.
Includes dedicated valet support and high-security monitoring.

### Disabled / Accessible Parking
Wheelchair-accessible bays near elevators on the ground floor. Requires a valid
disability permit. Extra-wide bays with elevator priority access. Free or deeply
discounted rates — see live pricing for current rates.

### Bike / 2-Wheeler Parking
Covered ground-floor zone for motorcycles, scooters, and electric two-wheelers.
Includes helmet safety lockers, EV bike charging points, and CCTV monitoring.

## Reservation Process

How to reserve a parking space via the chatbot:
1. Provide your full name
2. Provide your email address
3. Enter your vehicle registration number
4. Select parking type (Standard, Large, EV, VIP, Disabled, Bike)
5. Choose booking start date and time
6. Choose booking end date and time
7. Review and confirm all details

After confirmation:
- The reservation goes to admin review (usually within a few minutes)
- You will receive an email confirmation or rejection
- Approved reservations include your assigned slot number

Booking rules:
- Bookings must be placed at least 30 minutes before the desired start time
- Maximum single reservation: 30 days
- Free cancellation up to 2 hours before the booking starts
- Late cancellations (within 2 hours) may incur a partial charge

## Cancellation & Refund Policy

- Cancel any time via the chatbot or by contacting support
- Full refund if cancelled more than 2 hours before start time
- 50% refund for cancellations within 1–2 hours of start time
- No refund for no-shows or same-hour cancellations
- Monthly subscriptions: 7-day notice required for cancellation
- Refunds processed to original payment method within 5–7 business days

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is EV charging available?**
Yes. Floor P2 has a dedicated EV zone with both Level 2 AC chargers and DC fast-charge
ports. Charging is included in the EV parking fee. Open 24/7.

**Q: Is overnight parking allowed?**
Yes. The facility operates 24/7 and overnight parking is fully supported.
Book in advance to guarantee a spot during peak event days.

**Q: Can I cancel my reservation?**
Yes. Free cancellation up to 2 hours before your start time. See cancellation policy above.

**Q: Is valet parking available?**
Yes, during business hours (08:00–20:00). Valet is included for VIP Premium holders;
other customers can request it for an additional fee.

**Q: Are all parking floors covered?**
Yes. All six floors are covered and sheltered from rain and direct sunlight.

**Q: Is the parking facility secure?**
Yes. AI-CCTV, ANPR cameras, 24/7 guards, RFID access, fire systems, and emergency
SOS buttons are deployed throughout the facility.

**Q: Can I reserve parking online?**
Yes, through this AI chatbot. Just tell the assistant you want to book a parking space.

**Q: What is the maximum booking duration?**
30 days in a single reservation. Monthly subscription plans are available for
longer-term needs.

**Q: Are there monthly subscription plans?**
Yes — Silver, Gold, and Platinum corporate plans are available. Contact
corporate@parksmart.in or ask the chatbot for details.

**Q: Is a GST invoice available?**
Yes, for corporate accounts. Request it at the time of payment or email
support@parksmart.in with your booking reference.

**Q: What vehicles can use EV parking?**
Any registered electric vehicle — Tata Nexon EV, MG ZS EV, Tesla, BYD Atto 3,
Ather 450X, Ola S1 Pro, and others. Hybrid vehicles may park but cannot use
dedicated charging ports unless they are plug-in hybrids.

**Q: Is bike parking available?**
Yes — a dedicated covered zone for motorcycles, scooters, and e-bikes is on the
ground floor with helmet lockers and EV charging for two-wheelers.
