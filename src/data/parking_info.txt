# ParkSmart Hyderabad – Parking Information Database

## General Information

ParkSmart Hyderabad is a premium smart parking facility designed for professionals, employees, and visitors in Hyderabad’s HITEC City business district. Located near the EPAM Hyderabad Office and major IT hubs, ParkSmart provides secure, automated, and AI-enabled parking services for cars, electric vehicles, bikes, and corporate fleets.

The facility operates with modern smart parking infrastructure including:

* AI-powered parking management
* Automated entry/exit barriers
* License plate recognition system
* Real-time parking availability tracking
* 24/7 CCTV surveillance
* Smart EV charging stations
* Emergency assistance support

ParkSmart Hyderabad is especially designed for:

* IT employees
* Corporate visitors
* Daily commuters
* Long-term subscribers
* Event parking visitors

Total Parking Capacity:

* 650 Parking Slots
* 6 Parking Floors
* Dedicated EV & VIP Sections

# Reservation Process

To reserve a parking space:

1. Enter your full name
2. Provide email address
3. Enter vehicle registration number
4. Select parking type
5. Select booking start date & time
6. Select booking end date & time
7. Confirm reservation details

After reservation:

* Admin reviews request
* User receives email confirmation/rejection
* Approved reservations receive parking slot number

Booking Rules:

* Booking must be done at least 30 minutes in advance
* Maximum reservation duration: 30 days
* Free cancellation up to 2 hours before booking
* Late cancellation charges may apply
